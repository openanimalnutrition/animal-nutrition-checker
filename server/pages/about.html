Hosting

Hosting is paid for by DVM Consulting, Inc., the provisional steward (disclosure of interest). The site runs on Amazon Lightsail, a service of Amazon Web Services (AWS), in the United States (region us-east-1).

Privacy

  • The site uses no accounts, cookies, scripts or analytics, and loads nothing from other sites.
  • What you enter (the requirement, the nutrient values and the facts about the animal) is used to compute the result. In normal use the checker keeps no record of it, of an AI assistant's tool arguments, or of IP addresses: its log holds only its start-up and shutdown messages and, for a malformed request, a fixed line with no content. The one exception: if the checker fails unexpectedly, the log holds the error, which may include part of the request.
  • The result page holds your entries in hidden fields, so that "Change the inputs" and "Download JSON" can send them back to the checker, which returns them without keeping them.
  • Lightsail keeps the checker's log for 3 days, and the server's CPU and memory use for up to 15 months. It gives the steward no record of requests or IP addresses. AWS handles the connection data needed to deliver the site, under the AWS Privacy Notice.
  • Through an AI assistant, the assistant's provider calls the checker with the values its model chose; the checker treats them as any other request. What you type in the assistant is covered by that provider's terms, not by this site.
  • Issues on GitHub are public. If you email the contact address below, DVM Consulting keeps your message and address in order to answer you.

Contact
